MacroSync

Privacy

MacroSync collects only information needed to provide the service, protect users, and operate accounts.

Information we use

Account email, display name, declared date of birth, nutrition logs, progress measurements, friendships, messages, feedback, and moderation records may be stored to provide the features you request.

Age declaration

Your date of birth is used to determine whether age-restricted language rules apply. It is an age declaration, not identity verification, and you cannot change it yourself after declaration.

Food data

USDA food searches are retrieved from USDA FoodData Central. Community and personal food records are stored in MacroSync so they can be reused as intended.

Messages and moderation

Messages are stored so conversations work across devices. Automated non-AI moderation checks are used to block prohibited content and protect users. Administrators may review flagged content when moderation action is required.

Deletion

You can permanently delete your account from Settings → Privacy. Application data associated with the account is deleted through database cascade rules.
