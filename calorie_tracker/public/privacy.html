MacroSync

Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what MacroSync collects, why it is used, and how information is handled when you use MacroSync. MacroSync is designed so that user information is used to provide, secure, moderate, and improve MacroSync—not sold as a product to unrelated third parties.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, MacroSync may process account credentials and contact information, display-name information, date of birth, food logs, nutrition goals, progress information, foods you create, community foods, recipes, friendships, messages, reports, feedback, moderation records, and technical information needed to operate the service.

2. Why we use information

We use information to create and authenticate accounts, save food logs and food-database records, provide requested social and trainer features, enforce safety and content rules, respond to reports and support requests, protect the service from abuse, and maintain and improve MacroSync.

3. No sale of personal information

MacroSync does not sell personal information to third parties. MacroSync does not use personal information for unrelated third-party marketing or data-brokerage purposes. If this policy ever changes in a material way, the policy will be updated before the changed practice is introduced where legally required.

4. Third-party service providers

MacroSync relies on service providers to operate the app. For example, Supabase may provide authentication, database, and storage infrastructure, and other providers may be used for hosting, email delivery, payments, or similar operational services. These providers may process and, where technically necessary, store information on MacroSync's behalf. That processing does not give them ownership of the information or permission to use it for their own unrelated purposes.

5. Food database

USDA FoodData Central searches are requested so MacroSync can provide nutrition data. Community and personal food records are stored so users can reuse them within MacroSync.

6. Date of birth and age-based access

MacroSync requires a date of birth to enforce its age rules. Accounts are not available to users under 13. Users ages 13–15 receive a limited account and require parent or legal guardian consent. Limited accounts are restricted to food logging and the food database until the required consent status is approved. Users 16 and older may use the standard feature set subject to the Terms of Service.

7. Messages, friends, and moderation

Messages and friendship information are processed only to provide those features to eligible accounts. Automated, non-AI moderation may check content for prohibited language, personal contact information, and other safety concerns. Administrators may review reported or flagged content when necessary to enforce MacroSync's rules.

8. Data retention and deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet security and legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain required records. You may request or use available account-deletion tools to remove your account and associated application data, subject to information that must be retained by law or for legitimate security/legal purposes.

9. Security

MacroSync uses authentication controls, database access controls, and other reasonable safeguards appropriate to the service. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Privacy choices and requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or otherwise control certain information. Contact MacroSync using the support contact provided by the service to make a privacy request.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as MacroSync changes. Material changes will be identified by updating the version and effective date and, where appropriate, asking users to accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

Privacy questions or requests should be directed to the MacroSync support contact published in the app.
